Guitar Tab

Turn a song into guitar tab

Pick an audio or video file, paste a link, or record the song while it plays. The app pulls the guitar out of the mix, works out the tuning and where the capo is, and writes the tab. Everything runs on this device; nothing is uploaded.

Working on it

Starting
  1. Load the models
  2. Pull the guitar out of the mix
  3. Find the beat
  4. Check for a second guitar
  5. Hear the notes
  6. Work out tuning and capo
  7. Write the tab

Keep this screen open. On a phone, locking the screen or switching apps pauses the work.

Tap a note to move it to another string, change it, or delete it.
